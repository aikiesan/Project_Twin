|---|---|

## 4. Revenue stack (model scenarios)
1. Gas sale (buyer: distributor call / Mercado Cativo Verde / free market / CNG-LNG / own fleet)
2. CGOB (0 / 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.5 R$/m³)
3. CBIO (requires certification; **open legal question: stacking with CGOB on the same volume**)
4. Digestate (NPK value − logistics)
5. Avoided costs (diesel, NG/LPG, electricity)
6. Carbon credits (voluntary; additionality)
7. Tax effects (ICMS, REIDI, debentures)

## 5. Regulatory checklist for a new SP plant
1. CETESB LP → LI → LO (+ vinasse plan P4.231 if fertirrigating digestate)
2. ANP authorization (Res. 987/2025) + monthly SIMP reporting
3. Gas quality per ANP Res. 1.006/2026
4. ANP producer certification (ACO), bookkeeper, CGOB issuance (Res. 996/2026)
5. Optional RenovaBio certification (CBIOs)
6. Grid: ARSESP 744/1.342/1.765 — CUSD-Verde, TUSD-Verde, Bio-Citygate; or CNG/LNG sales
7. Commercial route: Comgás/Necta/Naturgy calls, Mercado Cativo Verde, free market
8. Taxes: ICMS (post-2026?), REIDI, debentures, IBS/CBS transition
9. Digestate registration (MAPA) if sold
10. Optional: SP certificate, voluntary carbon
