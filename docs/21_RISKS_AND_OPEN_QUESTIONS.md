## 2. Open questions

2. Will SP ICMS reduction (12 %) be renewed after 31/12/2026? Effects of IBS/CBS?
3. What are actual **TUSD-Verde** values?
4. 2027 CNPE target (due 1 Nov 2026)?
5. Status of the SP biomethane origin certificate.

7. Typical SP cane haul distance (peer-reviewed)? A model-derived reference now exists: straw-weighted road distance from cane cells to the nearest mill, P50 19.51 km (P25 12.91, P75 27.17, P90 35.22) on the OSM road graph (`straw_mill_road_km_*`, D, `esd_fl_espacial_outputs`). 8. Which reactor types do SP mill plants actually use (CSTR vs plug-flow vs UASB)? Public sources describe stirred vertical tanks + horizontal digester (Geo design) — inference only.
9. Do zero months in ANP data mean shutdown or missing reports?
10. Was Programa Paulista de Biogás (Decreto 58.659/2012) revoked?
11. How much mill biomethane goes to power under capacity-reserve contracts (LRCAP) instead of the gas market? Cocal won 9.2 MW for 15 years from Aug 2028 (digest 2026-10-06, S). It is a competing outlet in the supply curve and a separate revenue route (docs/16 §4).
12. Will the revised CBPMESP IT-29 set separation distances for biomethane production? If yes, they become an exclusion buffer in siting (docs/12 Step 2).
13. What is a sourced range for `ethanol_yield` (L ethanol per t cane) at SP mills? The registry holds a single derived value (81 L/t [D], Santa Adélia). In the Morris screen it moves biomethane as much as `vin_gen` and `vin_cod` (one-sided elasticity 0.81, docs/13 §7), but it cannot be ranked without a range. Candidates are the RenovaBio mill-year data (LAI R9) and UNICA's ethanol per tonne series. Do not invent ±x %.
14. Which paper is the manure BMP citation "Møller, Sommer, Ahring 2004" (`moller2004`)? The DOI recorded in the repo has the Journal of Environmental Quality prefix. A search summary, which is not a source, put the title in Biomass and Bioenergy. Neither placement is confirmed, so the six manure parameters that cite it (`cattle_slurry_*`, `swine_slurry_*`) have no checked reference. Check the DOI with library access before the manure strand of Phase 1.
15. Where does `bmp_fullscale` = 0.85 (0.70–1.0) come from? The cited Janke et al. 2019 has lab data only and prints no ratio (C17). Ask the registry owner. Also: which "Fuess et al. 2024 CEJ" was meant for `vin_so4` and `olr_highrate` (two CEJ candidates in `references.csv` `fuess2024`)?
16. Manure base-load v0 (`engine.supply.manure`) cannot run until four inputs are sourced. Each needs a citation in `references.csv`: (a) `swine_slurry_density` (t/m³, to turn the Embrapa 14 L/head/day into mass); (b) `poultry_droppings_gen` (kg per bird per day, layers and broilers separately if possible); (c) `cattle_manure_per_head`, a herd-average rate, or dairy-cow counts (SIDRA table 94) to use `dairy_manure`; (d) collectable (confined) shares per species for SP, e.g. from LUPA or Censo Agro 2017. No defaults are set.

19. Should the suitability screen let the São Paulo metro lead? With feedstock as N3 CH₄ within 30 km (ADR-0017, run `suitability_v0_ch4_20261008T113536Z`), the 15 best cells are in São Paulo and Guarulhos. Before deciding, (a) read the CH₄ share by residue group at those sites: urban residues (RSU, sludge, prunings) or cane; (b) check whether population enters twice, once in `demand` and once through urban residues; (c) decide on an urban-area exclusion (source to be registered). Siting a CSTR plant in dense urban land also meets land price and licensing limits that the screen does not model. 2026-10-08 breakdown: urban CH₄ is not the cause. Metro sites hold 44,000–202,000 Nm³/d within 30 km, against 716,000–924,000 in the cane belt. The metro leads on gas, power, road and population (4 of 5 equal weights), and the belt saturates at the CH₄ P99 bound (ADR-0017 notes). The question becomes the weight baseline and the role of `demand`.
