## Costs (EPE and others)
- EPE notes on investments and O&M costs in biofuels: OPEX factor for sugar-energy biomethane **R$ 0.150/Nm³** (recent edition); earlier reference **R$ 0.142/Nm³**.
- EPE: biomethane LCOB for a sugar-energy mill processing **2 Mt cane/yr: R$ 0.78–1.83/m³**; landfill biomethane **R$ 1.04–1.85/m³**.
- Existing biomethane projects: **770 thousand Nm³/d, CAPEX R$ 3.0 bi → R$ 3,734.9 per Nm³/d**.
- EPE: vinasse + filter cake potential in 2034 ≈ 6.4 bi Nm³ biogas ≈ 3.5 bi Nm³ biomethane; CAPEX ≈ R$ 31.5 bi. Feedstock parameters: **1 t filter cake = 90–120 Nm³ biogas; 1 m³ vinasse = 12–25 Nm³ biogas**. OPEX estimates based on BNDES data for a plant of 33 M Nm³/yr biogas and 18.2 M Nm³/yr biomethane.
