## 5. Methodology (all [K] — verify)
- AACE RP 18R-97: Class 5 (−20/−50 %, +30/+100 %), Class 4 (−15/−30 %, +20/+50 %), Class 3 (−10/−20 %, +10/+30 %).
- Six-tenths rule C2 = C1·(S2/S1)^n.
- Escalation: CEPCI (paid), INCC-M (FGV), IPCA (IBGE), IGP-M (FGV).
- Lang factors ~3.1 / 3.6 / 4.7 (Peters & Timmerhaus).
- International leads: IEA Outlook 2020 (https://www.iea.org/reports/outlook-for-biogas-and-biomethane-prospects-for-organic-growth) and 2025 update; IRENA biogas for road vehicles (2018); EBA statistical report; DBFZ; NREL 2013; Bauer et al. 2013 SGC 270; Angelidaki et al. 2018 (doi:10.1016/j.biotechadv.2018.01.011); Moraes, Zaiat & Bonomi 2015; Longati et al. 2020 (verify).
