**Caution on REIDI values (#22, #23, #33).** REIDI suspends PIS/Pasep and Cofins on goods and services bought for the project. The amounts in the portarias are the **qualifying goods and services**, which may differ from total CAPEX. For example, #22 says "considering taxes", and #23 says "goods and services with PIS/Cofins incidence". Treat them as an order-of-magnitude CAPEX until a primary document is read.
