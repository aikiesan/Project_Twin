## 2. Conflicts (log in `docs/21_RISKS_AND_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` §Conflicts; do not average)

1. **fc_bmp**: the registry high is 260, but Janke 2015 batch gives 245–281 NmL CH₄/g VS. Janke 2020 batch untreated gives 185, and its CSTR steady state gives 224. Values differ between batch campaigns and samples.
2. **bmp_fullscale**: 0.85 is attributed to Janke 2020. That paper reports continuous (224) ≥ batch (185/174/222) mL CH₄/g VS, a ratio of about 1.0–1.3. The 0.85 factor has no identified source.
3. **b0_cattle**: the registry central is 0.20. IPCC 2006 Latin America gives dairy 0.13 and other cattle 0.10 (S, secondary). 2019 Refinement snippets give 0.24 for high-productivity dairy and 0.13 for low-productivity, plus a paired pasture value of 0.19 (whep PR). Which productivity class fits SP dairy/feedlot is a modelling decision.
4. **vin_k2o**: the registry high is 4.6 g/L (mixed mash). Molasses vinasse reaches 3.74–7.83 g/L K₂O. Juice vinasse is 1.2–2.1 g/L, below the registry low of 1.5.
5. **vin_ch4_yield basis**: Janke 2015 gives 0.246–0.302 per g COD *added*. Barbosa 2022 gives 0.40 L per g COD *removed*, above the 0.35 Nm³/kg theoretical at 0 °C, so it is probably at reactor temperature. Units and reference T must be harmonised before use.
6. **ANP 1.006/2026 PCS regional grouping**: snippet A has North 34.0–38.4 and NE/CO/SE/S 35.0–43.0 MJ/m³. Snippet B has North+NE 34.0–38.4 and CO/SE/S 35.0–43.0. SP is unaffected (35.0–43.0 in both).
7. **HHV reference basis**: ANP uses 20 °C. CLAUDE.md "37–39 MJ/Nm³" is only consistent with ~93–98 % CH₄ on a 0 °C basis. Code must convert explicitly (×293.15/273.15 = 1.0732).
8. **IPCC B0 volume basis (K, verify)**: IPCC Eq. 10.23 converts m³ to kg CH₄ with 0.67 kg/m³, which matches ~20 °C rather than 0 °C (0.717 kg/Nm³). If confirmed, multiply IPCC B0 by ~0.93 to express it in Nm³ at 0 °C.
9. **opex_epe totals**: the snippet gives "R$ 5.0 bi OPEX 2026–2035 for biomethane". R02 recorded R$ 1.1 bi, with R$ 5.0 bi only for the full potential. The scope has to be resolved in the PDF.
10. **upg_elec_membrane high**: the registry high is 0.35. A second snippet gives two-stage membranes 0.2–0.38 kWh/Nm³ raw. Angelidaki's table gives 0.18–0.20.
11. **restart_days**: the 30 d value is for a **UASB**, not a CSTR. The registry high of 60 has no source.
12. **cod_so4_crit**: the central 10 interpolates the two tested ratios (12 and 7.5). It is not a reported threshold.
13. **vin_temp**: 90 °C (Volpi) vs 85–90 °C (secondary). This is a minor difference.
14. **haul_dist vs SUCRE**: the registry has 25 km. The CNPEM SUCRE straw scenarios assume a 35 km average radius (straw collection, so a different quantity, but a relevant benchmark).
15. **fc_gen low**: most sources give 30. Some give 20 kg/t.
