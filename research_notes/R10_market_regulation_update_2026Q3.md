## 2. Implications for the engine

| Topic | Implication / proposed treatment |
|---|---|
| **CGOB revenue 2026** | No CGOB had been issued by about end-Sep 2026. Decreto 12.614/2025 makes the 2026 obligation pro rata from the first issuance. **Set CGOB revenue to 0 for 2026 cash flows.** Scenarios start in 2027: 0 / 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.5 R$/m³ (doc 16), central 1.0. |
| **CGOB volume basis** | 1 CGOB = 100 m³. ANP Res. 1.006/2026 uses **20 °C, 1 atm** as its reference (R08). If the CGOB m³ uses the same basis, then 1 m³(20 °C) = 273.15/293.15 = **0.932 Nm³(0 °C)** (D). The difference is about 7 %. Verify the reference conditions in Res. 995/996 before converting `R$/CGOB → R$/Nm³`. |
| **2027 mandate (CNPE due 1 Nov 2026)** | Linear scaling of 181.7 M m³ per 0.5 % (D; assumes a constant NG-market base): 0.5 % → 182 M m³/yr (≈ 0.50 M m³/d); **1.0 % (statutory base) → 363 M m³/yr (≈ 1.0 M m³/d)**; **1.5 % (industry projection) → 545 M m³/yr (≈ 1.49 M m³/d)**. A 1.5 % target would **exceed national authorized capacity (1.37 M Nm³/d, Jul 2026)** before any eligibility or certification losses, which points to CGOB scarcity pricing (high case). At 1.0 % the target ≈ 73 % of authorized capacity. Run 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.5 % as mandate scenarios. |
| **CBIO** | Central R$ 25 (Jul 2026), low R$ 21.6 (D, implied historic low), high R$ 44.5 (Sep 2025 average). The RenovaBio 2027–36 targets (CP 232, closing 29 Oct 2026) may shift the level. Stacking CBIO with CGOB on the same volume is still unresolved, so keep a scenario switch. |
| **Diesel (fleet / avoided cost)** | Keep two bases. **Distribution** R$ 6.30/L (Jul 2026) sets wholesale parity. **Retail** R$ ≈ 7.10/L (Sep 2026; range 6.97–7.33) sets the avoided cost for own-fleet substitution. Diesel is +15 % y/y, which improves biomethane-for-diesel parity. |
| **NG molecule / industrial NG** | Molecule 1.90 (Jul) → ≈ 2.01 R$/m³ (D, Aug–Oct 2026 if +6 % applied). The next adjustment is 1 Nov 2026. The Brent bands narrow volatility for distributors that adhered; use a narrower NG-price range for 2026–27 but keep the band itself as a scenario. Industrial delivered NG: no 2026 update found, so keep 3.80. |
| **Biomethane price** | No newer Argus assessment was found. Keep FOB 3.28 R$/m³ (Jan 2026) and treat it as stale beyond Q4 2026. |
| **ICMS / IBS-CBS** | ICMS 12 % is confirmed (S) through 31-12-2026 under Decreto 69.268/2024. **No renewal found**, so the engine needs a 2027+ switch: renewed (12 %) vs lapse (full rate, value to verify; do not assume). From 2027, CBS replaces PIS/Cofins under a **monophasic ad rem** regime for biomethane, so model those taxes in **R$/m³, not % of price**. The rates are not yet published. ICMS phase-out runs 2029–2032 (K). |
| **Grid access (siting)** | TUSD-Verde values are still unpublished. Keep the interconnection (network km + Bio-Citygate) as producer CAPEX. Add a scenario layer for "Interliga SP" (Necta–Comgás, 115 km) and the Porto Ferreira–Araras and Campinas–Salto links. These raise network-offtake feasibility for mills in Necta territory (central/west SP). |
| **Ramp-up / capacity factor** | Onebio started at 50 % of nameplate. Add a ramp-up profile parameter (year 1 ≈ 50 %) separate from steady-state capacity factor. |
| **Supply context** | SP ≈ 700 k m³/d authorized across 9 plants, with 8–11 more in the queue and > 800 k m³/d targeted by Dec 2026. Brazil has 1.37 M Nm³/d authorized across 21 plants and produced 1.06 M m³/d in 2025. Use these numbers to check calibration (doc 13) against the ANP monthly data (PILAR-2b 05e). |
