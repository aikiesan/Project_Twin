## 2. What the items mean for the engine
| Topic | Implication |
|---|---|
| **Off-season strategy S1 (stored cake, partial load)** | Cocal won a 15-year capacity-reserve contract (LRCAP 2026, 9.2 MW, supply from Aug 2028). It says stored vinasse and filter cake allow year-round output [S]. This is commercial evidence for S1 over S0 (idle off-season) and S4 (shutdown and restart), and it fits Narandiba's 30–39 % off-season capacity factor in the ANP series. It does not settle the storage losses (φ_store, which waits on lab E1); ask Cocal for cake BMP loss data during storage. |
| **Competing outlet** | LRCAP turns biomethane into power under a fixed contract. It is a revenue route the stack in docs/16 §4 did not have, and a competing use of mill biomethane in the supply curve (docs/21 Q11). |
| **Manure base-load (skeleton step, S2)** | PPM 2025 is out (Sep 2026). Brazil: cattle −1.7 %, milked cows at the lowest level since 1979, poultry at a record; SP produces 22.5 % of the national egg output, and Bastos is the 2nd municipality [S]. The skeleton can run on the held 2024 series. Re-export 2025 when a machine reaches SIDRA. In western SP the base-load should be modelled with **layers and broilers**, with the NH₄⁺ and K⁺ limits, not with cattle manure as the default. Keep layers and broilers apart, since their N and K loads differ. |
| **Poultry share in a real plant** | Cocal Paraguaçu reportedly runs on 38 % poultry manure [S, URL missing, basis unknown]. If the basis is found, it bounds the manure share in S2. |
| **OFMSW (Phase 1b)** | The IEE/USP plant gives a local, measured yield: 120–180 Nm³ biogas per t at 50–65 % CH₄, and 90–117 m³ biomethane per t [S]. The two ranges do not agree at the low end (120 × 0.50 = 60 Nm³ CH₄/t, not 90), so read the source before using either. The plant is pilot scale (25 t/d, about R$ 10 M), so keep it out of the CAPEX curve fit. OFMSW has no seasonality but is municipal in scale: use it as a co-substrate near urban poles (Ribeirão Preto, Piracicaba, Campinas) or as a dedicated urban plant. |
| **Sewage sludge (Phase 1b)** | Sabesp planned 150,000 Nm³/d of biogas at three RMSP plants (2024 announcement, no update) [S]. Supply is concentrated far from the mills, so it matters more for an urban supply curve than for co-digestion with vinasse. |
| **Mill-level data** | The ANP RenovaBio certification panel is Power BI only, with no download. The data behind it (cane, ethanol, vinasse, NEEA, eligible volume per unit) is the main mill-level gap → LAI R9. |
| **CBIO scenarios** | CP 232/2026 (open until 29 Oct) sets the RenovaBio targets for 2027–2036: −12.1 % carbon intensity by 2036 (2018 base). The annual CBIO targets are in the supporting spreadsheets: download them to set the CBIO demand range. CBIO price stays at R$ 25.12 (Jul 2026 B3 average), in line with `price_cbio` = 25. |
| **Gas price anchors** | ARSESP custo-base from 10 Sep 2026: Comgás industrial R$ 2.629087/m³ and Necta R$ 2.370058/m³, both excluding taxes [S]. These are gas-cost components of the tariff, not delivered prices, so they do not replace `price_ng_ind` (3.80, delivered). |
| **Diesel** | Retail S10 R$ 7.35/L, national average for 27 Sep–3 Oct 2026 [S]. It is a retail value; `price_diesel` (6.30) is the distribution price. |
| **Offtake and siting** | The TransJordano corridor (100 biomethane trucks, open-access stations on Ribeirão Preto–Sumaré–Cubatão, R$ 140 M from BNDES) overlaps the cane belt. Its stations are candidate fleet and CNG offtake points for the siting model (docs/12 Step 4). It is also a case of Fundo Clima financing for the demand side. |
| **Avoided-methane credit** | The MBCBrasil study cites California LCFS values of about US$ 45/MMBtu for dairy-manure RNG against US$ 4.00–4.25/MMBtu for landfill gas [S]. It shows how much an avoided-methane credit could add to manure as a feedstock. Check whether the figure is the credit only or the total price before using it as a scenario bound. |
| **Phase 4 MILP** | OptBio (arXiv:2603.06823) is an open two-stage stochastic MILP with CVaR for Brazilian cane biorefineries, with piecewise-linear economies of scale. Read its formulation before writing the Phase 4 model. |
| **EPE CAPEX anchor (skeleton LCOB)** | The radar reads EPE NT 2025-08 as R$ 3,734.9 per Nm³/d **in R$ of Dec 2024**, labelled "sugar-energy biomethane". The price year is new for the registry. The label conflicts with our note "mix of landfill and agro" (docs/21 C15). This is verification target 1. |
